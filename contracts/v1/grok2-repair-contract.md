2. Later observations can advance mutable progress facts using effective/event time and source meaning. Ingestion time or a later timestamp alone is insufficient. Same-time contradictory claims remain visible as conflict; old values never appear alone as settled current truth after contrary accepted evidence.
3. Named issue resolution records the founder quote/answer and corrected state. New material contradictory evidence can reopen an issue; unrelated refresh and already-answered questions cannot.
4. Canonical questions and actual sent round items retain authorized evidence quotes/locators and actual selection reason, sufficient for the viewer to consume. Nora and Brain owners agree the smallest compatible shape before changing a shared schema; reuse existing evidence and question identities.

Pending business questions remain inspectable. Prefer supported consequential contradictions to generic gaps/reminders, with aging; never ask facts already explicitly established in relevant accepted evidence. A reminder cannot displace an unasked material conflict.
6. Message copy: recognizable permitted observation, one clear question, easy ordinary reply. No internal Q codes, paths, sample counts or repeated boilerplate. Evidence wording must be faithful and cannot disclose a participant's withheld title/content. Follow the report's human style, not its examples' accidental multiple-question structure.

Personal/excluded observations create no founder homework or private title exposure, including when a coverage-gap payload is projected. Unresolved permitted business context remains explicit.
8. A capture may yield a bounded number of distinct evidence-supported tasks and cite already-authorized accepted context sources. Link task output to another task's input only with evidence, never merely because a person/project name matches. Reuse known person identities; retain role qualifiers separately and do not merge homonyms.
